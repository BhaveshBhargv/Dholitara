Dholi Taara 5.0

Where tradition meets premium celebration

DHOLI TAARA 5.0 is Indore's most premium Garba celebration, bringing together culture, tradition, entertainment, and luxury on an unmatched scale. The event offers a world-class festive experience through high-energy Garba nights, immersive themes, premium hospitality, and exceptional production standards.

With a strong legacy of attracting families, corporates, and youth audiences, DHOLI TAARA has evolved into Central India's most sought-after Navratri destination, creating unforgettable experiences for over 1,00,000 attendees and valuable engagement opportunities for leading brands

Vision Ahead

At Events by B3, our vision for DHOLI TAARA is to create Central India's most iconic cultural entertainment property that celebrates the spirit of Navratri while setting new benchmarks in experience, production, and community engagement. We aspire to transform Garba from a traditional festival into a world-class celebration that brings together families, youth, corporates, artists, and brands on a single platform—preserving cultural heritage while delivering unforgettable festive experiences year after year.

Through innovation, inclusivity, and excellence, DHOLI TAARA aims to become a nationally recognized festival brand that proudly represents the cultural vibrancy of Indore.
